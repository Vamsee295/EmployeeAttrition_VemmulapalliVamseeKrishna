# 📖 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most expensive challenges organizations face.

Replacing skilled employees requires significant hiring costs, onboarding time, training expenses, and productivity loss.

This project applies **Machine Learning**, **Exploratory Data Analysis**, and **Business Intelligence** techniques to identify employees who are likely to leave and understand the reasons behind attrition.

The objective is not only to build a predictive model but also to provide actionable HR recommendations that help organizations retain valuable employees.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Can we identify employees who are likely to resign **before** they actually leave?

If yes,

- HR can intervene early.
- Employee retention improves.
- Hiring costs decrease.
- Productivity increases.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

## 🔥 Overtime

Employees working overtime show nearly **3× higher attrition** than those who do not.

---

## 💰 Salary

Employees leaving the organization earn approximately **38% less** than employees who stay.

---

## 📅 Employee Experience

Nearly **30%** of all resignations occur during the **first two years** of employment.

---

## 🏢 Department Analysis

The Sales department experiences the highest employee turnover.

---

## 👨‍💼 High-Risk Job Roles

- Sales Representative
- Human Resources
- Sales Executive
